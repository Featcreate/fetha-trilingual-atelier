# Tunisian seller account-provider choice

For the initial launch, seller onboarding is limited to sellers based in Tunisia. Each seller may choose one preferred provider for opening their merchant account:

- **Konnect** — use the platform's supplied referral registration link.
- **iPay** — visit the official iPay Tunisia website and contact the provider to request merchant onboarding.

The seller's choice is stored as an onboarding preference on the seller payout-preference record. It does not create an account on the provider's behalf, store provider credentials, verify a merchant account, route checkout, collect payments, or initiate payouts.

The current settlement workflow remains weekly manual transfers. No live payment or transfer capability is enabled by this choice. Provider eligibility, KYB requirements, commercial terms, and account approval are determined by the provider.

International seller registration is out of scope for the initial launch and may be added in a later platform phase after country coverage and provider support are verified.
